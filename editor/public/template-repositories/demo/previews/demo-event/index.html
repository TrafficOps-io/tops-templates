The programme

Leave room for the unexpected.

Short talks, open conversations, and time to make something with someone you’ve just met. Here’s the shape of our day.

  1. 10:00

    Arrive, meet, get curious

    Coffee, introductions, and a few questions to get the room talking.

  2. 11:00

    Ideas in the open

    Three perspectives on making things that bring people together.

  3. 14:00

    Make a little noise

    A hands-on workshop. Bring a question; leave with a first experiment.

  4. 16:30

    Keep the conversation going

    Share what you made, swap notes, and stay for the closing gathering.
